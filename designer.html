Clueboard

How it works

  • Click a square to write it. Drag it onto another square to swap the two.
  • To move a clue to another round, cut it and paste it there.
  • Click a category or a price to change it. Hover one to find its delete button.
  • Double-click a round's tab to rename it, and drag tabs to put the rounds in order. The Final Clue always comes last.
  • Paste a picture into an open square, or drop one onto any square. Videos go in as YouTube or Google Drive links.
  • Maths goes between dollar signs: $\frac{3}{4}$, $x^2$, $\sqrt{2}$.
